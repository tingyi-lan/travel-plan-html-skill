# Photo Output Guide

Use this reference for requested photography/content work or the relevant photo module of a full itinerary, as selected in `SKILL.md`. Photography remains a core capability for trips with photo value. Load only the needed sections for a caption, device tip, outfit suggestion, or post-trip album; those requests do not require a complete shooting plan, itinerary, or website.

## Core Principle

For a complete shooting plan or an itinerary's photo module, cover the following for each relevant core attraction or photogenic destination. For a focused request, answer only the requested aspects:

- Where to shoot.
- What time to shoot.
- How to stand or move.
- How to compose.
- What to wear.
- What props to bring.
- What device or lens to use.
- What settings or shooting mode to try.
- What weather, cloud, crowd, wind, safety, permit, or legal limits matter.
- What to do if conditions fail.
- What AI or post-trip output can be made from the photos.

Never reduce the photo module to "this place is good for photos". Give an executable shot plan.

In a full travel website with a photo module, place a classic photo check-in overview on the homepage, then repeat relevant spot-level photo cards inside each daily page. This layout does not require a standalone photo/content request to become a website. Default to phone-first advice when devices are unknown, while still recommending spot-specific equipment when it clearly helps.

## Device-Specific Guidance

### Phone Only

Give phone-first instructions for most users:

- Use grid lines, tap-to-focus, exposure lock, HDR, portrait mode, live photo/burst, panorama, night mode, or long exposure when suitable.
- Prefer optical lens choices: 0.5x for architecture/interiors, 1x for natural view, 2x/3x/5x for portrait compression if available.
- Avoid heavy digital zoom unless there is no alternative.
- Suggest simple settings: lower exposure slightly for bright sky/snow/lake; hold steady for night mode; use a mini tripod for night scenes.
- Give exact framing instructions, such as "person stands on lower-left third, gate fills upper two-thirds".

### DJI Drone

Only recommend drone shooting after checking legality and safety:

- Verify no-fly zones, scenic-area rules, local law, wind, altitude, people density, battery, takeoff/landing space, and insurance/risk.
- Use drone for coastline, lakes, desert, grassland, terraces, snow mountain approach, and road-trip scale shots.
- Suggest shot types: top-down, reveal, orbit, pull-back, legal follow-car, and wide establishing shot.
- Do not recommend illegal or unsafe flight.

### DJI Pocket / Gimbal

Use for walking video and stabilized travel clips:

- Plan 5-10 second clips: entrance push-in, walking follow, food close-up, hotel room sweep, family hand-hold shot, doorway transition.
- Use slow walk, horizon level, face tracking, hyperlapse, and one-take route segments.
- Avoid filming where privacy, religious etiquette, or crowd control makes it inappropriate.

### Action Camera / GoPro

Use for movement and harsh environments:

- Good for hiking, cycling, skiing, water, rafting, desert, snow, road trip, kids' POV, and hands-free family moments.
- Recommend chest/head/handle mounting only when safe.
- Mention battery, waterproof case, anti-fog, lens wipe, and stabilization.

### Mirrorless / DSLR

Use when the user owns camera gear:

- Match lens to purpose: wide angle for architecture/starry sky; standard zoom for flexible travel; telephoto for skyline compression, snow mountain, wildlife, or stage/temple details; fast prime for portraits/night.
- Include simple settings when relevant: aperture priority for portraits, faster shutter for kids/action, tripod and manual focus for stars.
- For night sky, aurora, comet, or Orion shots, check cloud cover, moon phase, light pollution, wind, and tripod stability.

## Destination Pattern Examples

Use these as pattern memory, not as unverified live facts:

- Fushimi Inari-style shrine corridors: plan very early morning, use leading lines, let the subject stand at a curve or gap, avoid blocking worshippers, choose simple clothing that contrasts with red/orange gates.
- Potala Palace-style landmark viewpoint: identify the classic banknote or postcard angle, verify current viewpoint access, use telephoto compression when available, and plan blue hour or early morning.
- Suzhou garden: use moon gates, windows, corridors, water reflections, pavilions, oil-paper umbrella, Chinese-style clothing, and quiet side paths; avoid oversized props that disturb visitors.
- Chaka Salt Lake / Uyuni-style reflection: check water depth, wind, sky clarity, and footwear; use bright or high-contrast outfits, reflection symmetry, silhouette, and forced perspective.
- Yulong Snow Mountain or other golden-mountain sunrise scenes: check sunrise time, cloud/visibility, viewing direction, temperature, altitude risk, and backup viewpoints.
- Aurora, Milky Way, comet, Orion, or starry sky: check cloud cover, aurora/Kp forecast where relevant, moon phase, light pollution, freezing wind, tripod, wide lens, manual focus, and warm batteries.

## Outfit And Props

Outfits must be useful, not just pretty:

- Match colors to scene: white/cream for gardens and coast, red/yellow for snow/lakes/desert, blue/green for city or temples when culturally appropriate, black/navy for night city, local or traditional clothing where respectful.
- Respect local culture, religious sites, memorials, dress codes, cold/heat, walking difficulty, and shoe comfort.
- Recommend layers for dawn/night shoots.
- Props should serve the photo: oil-paper umbrella, scarf, hat, bouquet, transparent umbrella, small lantern, picnic cloth, or parent-child matching item.
- Avoid props that are unsafe, disruptive, illegal, or inappropriate for the place.

## Travel Photography Services

When photo goals are high, judge whether paid help is worth it:

- No photographer: enough for phone-first casual output.
- Local travel photographer: useful for family/couple portraits in complex locations.
- Charter + photographer: useful when spots are dispersed or timing is tight.
- Photographer + makeup/styling: useful for wedding-like, costume, or high-output social trips.
- Equipment rental: useful for drone, camera, tripod, fill light, or winter/night gear when buying is wasteful.

Use public social/travel experience when available, especially Xiaohongshu, to judge local travel photography. Extract patterns only; do not copy creators' sample copy or images.

Always list:

- Style fit: documentary, couple portrait, family, parent-child, ethnic/local costume, cinematic, Xiaohongshu-style, wedding-like, or natural phone-style.
- Package contents: shooting duration, number of locations, raw files, retouched photos, makeup/styling, clothing, props, transport, tickets, drone/video, and assistant.
- Cost risks: overtime, extra retouching, add-photo fees, raw-file fees, costume/makeup upgrades, transport, entrance tickets, bad-weather rescheduling, shopping stops, and copyright/usage rights.
- Service risks: sample-photo mismatch, photographer communication, route compression, forced pose style, waiting time, refund/change policy, privacy, and commercial use of user portraits.
- Fit judgment: no paid shoot, local photographer, charter + photographer, photographer + makeup/styling, or equipment rental.

When recommending travel photography, explain which day/time slot it fits, which itinerary parts should be simplified, and what the non-paid phone-first alternative is.

## AI Output Module

Add a post-trip AI output plan when the user cares about photos or sharing:

| Output | What to prepare | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retouched photos | 3-10 best originals, desired style, skin/sky/color requirements | Retouch brief or edited assets when tools are available. |
| Nine-grid | 9 photos with varied wide/mid/close/detail/person shots | Posting order, cover image, caption rhythm. |
| Travel poster | Hero image, title, dates, destination, mood | Poster copy and generation/editing prompt. |
| Short-video template | 10-20 clips, music mood, aspect ratio | Shot order, transition plan, subtitles. |
| Social caption | Trip theme, people, emotions, facts to mention | Caption, title, hashtags, photo order. |

Do not claim AI retouching is complete unless image-editing tools were actually used. If the user has not uploaded photos yet, provide an upload checklist and editing brief.

## Daily Photo Deliverables

Every day with scenic or social value should include:

- 1-3 must-shoot frames.
- 1 family/group frame when relevant.
- 1 short-video or motion clip idea.
- Outfit/prop note for the day.
- Device note for phone users and optional advanced gear.
- Weather/crowd fallback.
- AI output role, such as cover image, nine-grid detail, poster hero, or video transition.

Do not let photo output damage the trip. Mark places that are not worth extra detours only for photos, limit daily must-shoot spots, and preserve rest, meals, safety, and normal sightseeing.

## Verification

Current shooting advice can depend on access, crowd control, permits, construction, opening hours, weather, sun direction, moon phase, cloud cover, aurora forecast, and no-fly zones. Verify when possible. Mark unknowns as `出发前需核验`.
